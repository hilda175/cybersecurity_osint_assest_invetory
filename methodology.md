# Methodology and scope

## Scope
This demonstration uses fictional asset records only. It does not query the internet, scan hosts, or interact with any real organization.

## Workflow
1. List each fictional asset and the observation available.
2. Record the service or technology clue without assuming it proves a vulnerability.
3. Assign a preliminary risk level based on the sample evidence.
4. Explain the uncertainty and recommend defensive verification.
5. Document limitations and next steps.

## Important limitation
A service banner can be inaccurate, incomplete, or deliberately altered. A version string by itself is not proof of exploitability. Risk ratings in this demo are illustrative and should not be treated as CVSS scores or a substitute for a professional assessment.
